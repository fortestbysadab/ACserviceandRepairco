Talk to support
How long does an AC service take?
A power-jet service takes about 1 hour per AC, and a foam-jet deep clean about 1.5 hours per AC. Multi-AC bookings are done in one visit, so two ACs typically finish in 2 hours.
What if my AC stops cooling again after the service?
Every repair carries a 90-day warranty on parts and labour. If the same fault returns, we return and fix it free — no revisit charge. Deep-clean services include a 7-day workmanship guarantee.
Do you use genuine spare parts?
Yes. We fit OEM or OEM-equivalent parts from brands including Daikin, Voltas, LG, Hitachi, Blue Star and Carrier. You'll see the part price in the app before fitting, and every part comes with a printed warranty slip.
Which refrigerant gases do you support?
R32, R410A and R22 for both split and window ACs. Your technician checks the nameplate first and uses only the gas your unit is designed for, with calibrated manifold gauges.
Are there any hidden charges?
No. The amount you see at checkout is what you pay. Anything outside the listed scope — like extra copper piping beyond 3 m — is quoted and approved by you in the app before the technician proceeds.
Can I reschedule or cancel my booking?
Reschedule free of charge up to 2 hours before your slot, as many times as you like. Cancel up to 2 hours before and you're refunded in full to the original payment method within 3–5 working days.
Do you service window ACs as well?
Yes — window AC deep clean, power-jet, installation, gas charging and repair are all available. Window units are pulled out of the frame for a full wash, then resealed and tilted correctly for drainage.
What does the Care plan cover that a one-off service doesn't?
Scheduled deep cleans across the year, free labour on unlimited repair visits, priority same-day slots in peak summer, discounts on spares, and — on Care+ — an annual gas refill. You can cancel within 15 days for a full refund.
